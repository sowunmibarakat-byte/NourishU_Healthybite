Managing Diabetes the Nigerian Way

Living with diabetes doesn’t mean you can’t enjoy Nigerian food. The key is choosing low GI foods, controlling portions, and eating on time. This helps prevent blood sugar spikes and keeps your energy stable.

✅ FOODS TO EAT REGULARLY

  • Vegetables: Ugu, Efo, Okra, Bitterleaf, Cabbage. Eat plenty! They have fiber that slows sugar absorption.
  • Protein: Fish, Chicken breast, Eggs, Beans, Tofu. Protein keeps you full and balances blood sugar.
  • Low GI Carbs: Oats, Brown rice, Sweet potato, Unripe plantain, Yam. These release sugar slowly.
  • Healthy Fats: Avocado, Groundnuts, Almonds. They help with insulin function.
  • Drinks: Water, Unsweetened tea, Zobo without sugar.
💡 Pro Tip: Use the "Plate Method". 1/2 plate vegetables, 1/4 plate protein, 1/4 plate carbs. This works for Jollof rice too!

❌ FOODS TO LIMIT OR AVOID

  • Sugary drinks: Coke, Fanta, Malt, Packaged juice. They spike blood sugar fast.
  • Refined carbs: White bread, White rice, Indomie. Replace with brown or whole grain versions.
  • Processed snacks: Biscuits, Cakes, Meat pie. High in sugar and bad fats.
  • Alcohol: Can cause dangerous blood sugar drops.

Managing High Blood Pressure Naturally

High BP is called the "silent killer" but food can be your medicine. The DASH diet works perfectly with Nigerian foods. We’ll reduce sodium, increase potassium, and protect your heart.

✅ FOODS TO EAT REGULARLY

  • Potassium-Rich Fruits: Banana, Orange, Pawpaw, Watermelon, Avocado. Potassium helps lower BP.
  • Leafy Greens: Spinach, Ugu, Garden egg leaf. Packed with magnesium.
  • Whole grains: Oats, Brown rice, Millet. High in fiber.
  • Lean Protein: Fish, Skinless chicken, Beans. Avoid fatty meats.
  • Nuts & Seeds: Groundnuts, Almonds. Good for heart health.
💡 Pro Tip: Cook with fresh pepper, ginger, garlic and herbs instead of Maggi. Your food will taste better and be healthier!

❌ FOODS TO LIMIT OR AVOID

  • Too much salt: Maggi, Seasoning cubes, Stock cubes. Use natural spices instead.
  • Processed meat: Sausage, Hotdog, Titus fish in brine, Corned beef. Very high in sodium.
  • Canned foods: Beans, Tomatoes. Rinse well if you must use.
  • Alcohol & Excess caffeine: Can raise blood pressure.

Healing Ulcer with Food

Ulcer pain is terrible, but the right foods can sooth your stomach lining and help you heal. The goal is to avoid irritants and eat on a consistent schedule.

✅ FOODS TO EAT REGULARLY

  • Gut Soothers: Oatmeal, Plantain, Potatoes, Yam, Fufu. Gentle on the stomach.
  • Lean Protein: Fish, Chicken breast, Eggs, Beans. Helps repair tissue.
  • Low-Acid Fruits: Banana, Pawpaw, Watermelon. Avoid citrus.
  • Vegetables: Carrot, Cabbage, Cucumber, Ugu. Cooked is better.
  • Probiotics: Plain yoghurt, Nunu. Good bacteria for gut health.
💡 Pro Tip: Eat small meals 4-5 times daily. Never sleep on an empty stomach. Have a banana before bed.

❌ FOODS TO LIMIT OR AVOID

  • Spicy food: Pepper soup, Hot sauce, Excess pepper. Major irritant.
  • Acidic foods: Orange juice, Lemon, Excess tomato paste.
  • Irritants: Coffee, Alcohol, Carbonated drinks, Fried foods.
  • Skipping meals: This increases stomach acid and pain.
